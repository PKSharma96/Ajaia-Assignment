# Product Launch Plan

This file shows what a **Markdown** import keeps: headings, **bold**, _italic_, <u>underline</u> and lists.

## Goals

- Launch the internal document editor
- Get **10 teams** using it in the first month
- Keep _every_ document safe with autosave

## Timeline

1. Week 1: internal beta
2. Week 2: fix feedback
3. Week 3: company-wide launch

### Notes

Tables and images are not supported by the editor, so only their text would be kept.
